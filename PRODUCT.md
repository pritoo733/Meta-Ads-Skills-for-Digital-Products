# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders, creators, and marketers selling digital products who want to use Meta Ads to acquire customers and improve sales.

## Product Purpose

An open-source Agent Skill repository that helps users plan and improve Meta Ads for SaaS, PDFs and downloads, video courses, paid communities, templates, and plugins. Success means users can understand the skill's value, install it, and use its product-specific guidance to make better evidence-based decisions toward profitable sales. Sales are the goal; no outcome is guaranteed.

## Positioning

Product-specific campaign guidance that follows the path from offer and economics through ad, checkout, purchase, and retention instead of defaulting to an agency booked-call funnel.

## Operating Context

The GitHub repository is published. The README is its primary discovery surface and should help visitors quickly understand the skill, choose the relevant product playbook, and install or explore it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product models: SaaS, PDFs/downloads, video courses, paid communities/memberships, templates, and plugins.
- Focus: Meta Ads only, with product-appropriate purchase, contribution, activation, refund, and retention measures.
- No verified customer testimonials, performance results, or brand assets have been supplied. Do not invent them.
- The README is rendered in GitHub's Markdown environment; custom CSS and interactive web behavior are not available.
- Do not promise guaranteed sales, ROAS, or specific outcomes.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-specified product categories and objective are in the current conversation.
- The related agency Meta Ads skill repository provides a structural reference only; its agency claims and booked-call path are not evidence for this product.
- No user results or testimonials are available for this skill yet.

## Product Principles

- Tie recommendations to seller-specific economics and evidence.
- Optimize for business outcomes beyond cheap clicks or platform-reported conversions.
- Adapt the funnel and measurement to the product model.
- State assumptions and limits plainly; never fabricate proof.
