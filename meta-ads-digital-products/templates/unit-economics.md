## Sanity checks

- `Platform ROAS = platform-attributed purchase value ÷ platform-reported spend`
- `Net revenue ROAS = net collected cohort revenue ÷ ad spend`
- `Contribution ROAS = contribution before acquisition ÷ ad spend`
- `Affordable customers at target = spend cap ÷ allowable media CAC`

Affordable customers at target is conditional arithmetic, not a forecast. Do not count unobserved future renewals as realized contribution. Avoid subtracting the same fees or acquisition costs twice.
