Make concept hypotheses meaningfully different in buyer motivation, use case, mechanism, objection, or proof. Record the product element shown and avoid treating a new crop or caption as a new strategic angle by itself. Do not create more variants than the budget can meaningfully evaluate.

Before publishing, confirm product accuracy, rights/permissions, disclosures, policy fit, caption/readability without sound, placement crops, URL, price/terms, and event path.
