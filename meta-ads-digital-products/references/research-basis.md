# Research basis

Reviewed 14 October 2026. These sources inform the general principles in this skill; they are not performance guarantees, universal benchmarks, or a substitute for current account-specific documentation.

## Official Meta guidance

- [Increase campaign performance with diversified creative](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/253130-increase-campaign-performance-with-diversified-creative) — Meta Blueprint describes diversification by concept, buyer motivation, and format. Applied here as distinct creative hypotheses, with budget and measurement limits made explicit.
- [Maximize performance with Advantage+ sales campaigns](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/253126-advantage-plus-sales-course) — Meta Blueprint covers data integration, creative diversification, and measurement. Verify current campaign features and availability in the actual account before recommending setup.
- [Selecting campaign objectives and conversion settings](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/253165-selecting-objectives-course) — Meta Blueprint connects objectives with conversion locations, events, and performance goals. Choose around the business outcome and what the account can measure reliably.

## Practitioner material and case reports

- [Jon Loomer, “Make Creative Testing Part of Your Process”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b6_4nv_nLPU) ([episode page](https://pubcast.jonloomer.com/videos/make-creative-testing-part-of-your-process/)) — the public episode description discusses Meta's creative testing tool and staged comparisons instead of testing many ads at once. The player and transcript were unavailable during this review; only the public description was reviewed. Check current account availability before using that feature.
- [UM, digital education case study](https://um.marketing/blog/cases/digital-education/) — the agency describes focusing the product catalog, rebuilding creative/testing, improving the landing path, and scaling gradually. The result figures are agency-reported, not independently verified; they are not used as a forecast or target.
- [Triple Scale, digital course case study](https://www.scaletriple.com/project/from-0-to-130k-total-ad-spend-with-a-digital-course) — the agency describes a creative-first approach and links a YouTube video. Its performance claims are self-reported, and the embedded video was not independently reviewed here. Use the example only to motivate testing new concepts, not to infer likely ROAS or spend requirements.

## How to use these sources

Prefer current official documentation for platform features, event configuration, and policy. Treat platform training as guidance about the platform, not proof that an approach will be profitable for a particular seller. Treat practitioner examples and public case studies as hypotheses to investigate. Ground decisions in the seller's captured payments, refunds, contribution, customer quality, funnel counts, and mature cohorts. Re-check time-sensitive claims before use.
