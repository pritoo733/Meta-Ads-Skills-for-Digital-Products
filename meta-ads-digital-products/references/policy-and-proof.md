# Policy, privacy, and proof

Meta ad controls, advertising standards, measurement products, and local privacy obligations change. For a live campaign, check the current official documentation and the actual account before giving configuration-specific advice.

## Claims and substantiation

- Verify claims against product documentation, representative user evidence, and the approved offer.
- Avoid guaranteed earnings, savings, health, career, business, or performance results unless the specific claim has adequate support and the applicable policy permits it.
- Do not imply knowledge of a viewer's sensitive traits or condition. Use neutral, product-relevant language.
- Present testimonials and case studies with permission and material context; never fabricate, edit into a different meaning, or present an exceptional result as typical.
- Match ad claims to the landing page, checkout, product experience, and terms. Explain limitations that materially affect the decision.

## Data and tracking

Use only data the seller is allowed to collect and share. Check consent, data minimization, notice, retention, access, and applicable local privacy rules. Do not send sensitive data or secrets in URLs, event parameters, or prompts. Hashing does not itself establish permission or make an otherwise prohibited data use acceptable.

When Pixel and Conversions API are used together, follow Meta's current implementation and deduplication guidance and verify test events. Do not assume that a server event is automatically accurate or compliant. Keep the payment/order system as the commercial source of truth for completed and refunded sales.

## Current-source checklist

Before advising live setup or a policy-sensitive claim, review:

- [Meta Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/)
- [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help)
- [Meta Pixel documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/meta-pixel/)
- [Conversions API documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/)
- The relevant platform/store requirements and local privacy rules for the seller's market.

State the source and date checked for any time-sensitive conclusion. If official documentation is inaccessible, say what could not be verified and keep the recommendation general.
