# Product-model playbooks

Choose the buyer path and quality signal that fits the product. These are prompts for analysis, not universal campaign settings.

## SaaS and software subscriptions

Map signup/trial → activation → paid conversion → renewal. Define activation as a meaningful product action, not simply account creation. Segment by acquisition cohort, trial type, plan, billing cadence, and cancellation/refund window. Evaluate CAC against observed contribution and payback; treat signups as an upstream signal unless the business goal is explicitly a qualified trial.

Check onboarding friction, time to value, team/integration requirements, product readiness, free-to-paid terms, and sales-assisted steps. If paid volume is low, diagnose activation and payment conversion before assuming the ad is the only issue.

## PDFs and one-time downloads

Clarify the exact artifact, intended use, preview, delivery, license, compatibility, and refund terms. Inspect product-page visits → checkout starts → completed paid orders. Compare the order contribution after marketplace/payment fees, discounting, refunds, and support. Bundles or order bumps are hypotheses; use observed incremental contribution and refund impact.

## Video courses and education

Specify learner, starting point, curriculum, access, support, schedule, outcomes, prerequisites, and refund/payment-plan terms. Match the consideration period to the offer and purchase window; do not evaluate early leads against a mature purchase cohort. Use approved learner evidence with context and permission. Avoid guaranteed earnings, career, health, or other outcomes without valid substantiation and policy review.

## Communities and memberships

Separate acquisition from activation, participation, renewal, cancellation, and retained contribution. A cheap initial join is not proof of member value. Be clear about recurring billing, moderation, access, schedule, cancellation, and refund terms. Review cohort retention after the relevant renewal period and account for community capacity.

## Templates and digital assets

Show the actual template in use and state supported tools, formats, versions, license, update policy, and support. Look for confusion at preview, compatibility, download, and first-use stages. Evaluate refunds and support requests as quality signals alongside checkout conversion.

## Plugins, extensions, and add-ons

State the host platform, supported versions, permissions, setup needs, limitations, and update/support commitments. Trace click → install → activation → trial or paid state → renewal where relevant. Distinguish store installs from active, paying customers. Review store approval, platform requirements, privacy disclosures, and technical onboarding before scaling.

## Picking the route

Prefer a direct product page and checkout when the offer is easy to understand and buy. Use a demo, trial, webinar, or longer explanation only when it resolves a real buyer question or reduces meaningful fit friction. The additional step must be measured against purchase quality and contribution, not judged by its own view or registration count.
